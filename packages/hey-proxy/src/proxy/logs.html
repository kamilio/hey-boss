Today starts at your local midnight. Weeks start Monday in your local time zone. Spend follows each request’s start time and includes all retained local history, including reported usage from requests still running. Refreshes every five seconds.

Estimates use recognized models’ standard list prices and reported usage, including cache reads and writes. Unknown models and missing usage remain unpriced. Claude subscription usage shows its API-equivalent value, not additional subscription charges. Subscription fees, discounts, tool fees and unreported retry charges are excluded. Logging gaps can undercount spend. No prompts, response bodies or credentials are stored.
